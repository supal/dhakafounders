1. Hex Color Palette

Primary (Sky Blue): #5CB3F9 (Used in the hero gradient and button highlights, creating an energetic and modern tech feel).

Secondary (Dark Slate): #1A2530 (Used for the dark background sections like "Our Services" and the footer, providing high contrast and a premium look).

Accent (Button Blue): #2572B4 (Used for primary calls-to-action to stand out against both light and dark backgrounds).

2. Google Fonts

Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans or Montserrat (These geometric sans-serif fonts perfectly match the wide, clean, and modern aesthetic of the headings like "Dhaka Founders" and "Build. Ship. Scale.").

Body: Inter (A highly legible, versatile sans-serif that fits the clean UI standard for modern SaaS and directory descriptions).

3. Tone of Voice

Collaborative & Supportive: Phrases like "Connecting Bangladeshi founders" and "support each other's growth" establish a community-first, welcoming environment.

Action-Oriented & Ambitious: The punchy "Build. Ship. Scale." copy drives momentum, appealing to the fast-paced nature of startup founders and tech builders.

Professional yet Accessible: The language is clear, concise, and avoids heavy jargon, making the network feel both high-quality and approachable.
